# Glossa UX and accessibility audit

Audit of the current UI (demo mode, served over http) before the visual redesign. Method: code read of `index.html`, `css/*.css`, `js/app.js`, `js/ui/*.js`; headless Chrome at 1280×800 and 380×800 (Queue with a card open, Auto-approved, Publish, Report); measured contrast from the CSS tokens, tap targets via `getBoundingClientRect`, horizontal overflow, real Tab key presses, and timings. Rules come from the UI UX Pro Max quick reference (priority categories 1–10) and WCAG 2.2 AA.

## Summary

The core review loop works: the review card fits 1280×800 with no page scroll, the A/E/R/C shortcuts work and show their keys, every decision can be undone, and tier is never shown by colour alone. The three most important problems are these. (1) **Destructive reset with no confirmation.** "Reset demo" in the footer wipes every decision with one click, and in live mode it also replaces the live run. (2) **Keyboard navigation breaks around the tabs and the list.** Activating a tab sends focus to `<body>` because the tab bar is rebuilt on every store change. The tabs have no arrow-key support. Each queue row is its own Tab stop, so it takes 34 Tab presses to get from the first row to Accept (about 145 on a 140-entry live queue). (3) **The numbers disagree from view to view.** The hero says "140 need you" and "3,000 concepts". The Queue says 30 pending, Publish says it covers a "40-entry sample", and Auto-approved says "Showing 10 of 2,860". A linguist can't tell how much work is left, and a hiring viewer will spot it. Low-contrast secondary text (#8a8a85 at 3.26–3.47:1) and near-invisible control borders (1.24:1) are the main visual accessibility failures.

## Keep (the redesign must not break these)

- **The card fits at 1280×800 with no page scroll.** The card ends at y=719 and Accept at y=700. With the reject form open it ends at 738, and at 1366×768 it still ends at 719. The sticky list and card columns (`review.css:28-30`) make this work.
- **Tier is never shown by colour alone.** Badges carry a text label. Judge checks use ✓/✗/— glyphs plus visually hidden yes/no words. The confidence bar shows a percentage. Status badges are text.
- **The focus ring is visible on every stop tested** (2px #6366f1, 4.20:1 on `--bg`, 3.97:1 on the selected row). The skip link appears at the top when focused.
- **The Tab order from the header is logical:** tabs → tier chips → random-check toggle → "Why this order?" → rows → card controls → actions.
- **Shortcuts.** A/E/R/C work with visible `<kbd>` hints, are ignored inside inputs and selects, and Escape leaves a form. Edit, Reject and Ask autofocus their first field and keep the draft text.
- **Every decision is reversible.** Undo is kept per entry (`state.js`) and shown on decided cards. Auto-approved entries can be reopened. A Critical override escalates and says so in the toast.
- **No horizontal page overflow at 380px on any view** (`scrollWidth` 380).
- **Most text passes contrast.** Body text 15.9:1, `--text-2` 6.0–6.8:1, accent links 5.54:1, tier badges 4.71–6.79:1 (high is the tightest), white on accent 5.54:1, toast 15.9:1.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` is respected globally (`app.css:263`, `review.css:192`).
- **The empty states say what to do next** ("Queue clear … Check the Auto-approved tab, or clear a filter"; "Review a few entries in the Queue and this section fills in").
- **The Report's enforcement preview** (source and translation side by side, highlighted terms, "✓ used (hard)" chips, the retried first attempt) is the clearest explanation of the product. Keep it.
- **The Publish tables are semantic** (`th scope="row"/"col"`, `<details>` groups with counts). The CSV export guards against formula injection (`publish.js:9-13`).
- **The console is clean** apart from the expected `api/health` 404.

## Design implications for the restyle (ivory #FBF7EC, emerald #047857, Plus Jakarta Sans, GSAP)

- **Emerald clashes with the Low tier and with success.** Emerald #047857 against the Low/DNT tier colour #067647 measures **1.04:1**, so they are effectively the same colour. The same green is also used for `--ok` (Accepted/Edited badges, "✓ used", "Decided" tile, the high confidence bar) and for `--seg-auto`. If emerald becomes the brand and accent, "brand", "Low tier" and "accepted" all read alike. Move the Low/DNT tier to a non-green hue (slate or teal-blue), and keep emerald for chrome and primary actions.
- **The proposal in brand colour.** Today the drafter's proposal (28px), the target renderings in the tables and the Auto-approved targets all use `--accent`. In emerald a proposal will look already approved. Use ink (#1d1d1b) or a neutral strong colour for target-language text.
- **Light fills disappear on ivory.** Against #FBF7EC: white surface **1.07:1**, `--surface-2` **1.06**, the High-tier fill #fdf3e1 **1.03**, the Standard-tier fill **1.06**, `<mark>` #fff1b8 **1.06**, `--border` #e8e7e2 **1.16**. Badges and chips placed straight on ivory lose their pill shape. Keep badges on white panels or give them a 1px tinted border. Make the context `<mark>` a stronger yellow, or underline it.
- **Muted text on ivory.** `--text-3` falls to **3.24:1** on ivory, so it must be darkened (#6b6a64 gives 5.07:1). `--text-2` stays fine at 6.37:1. The High tier text stays at **4.84:1** on ivory, which is the tightest badge pair.
- **Control borders on ivory.** They need a strong border token: #949088 is only 2.97:1 on ivory, so go slightly darker on ivory, or keep inputs on white with #8f8b80 (3.40:1).
- **The emerald pairs pass.** Emerald on ivory is 5.12:1, white on emerald 5.48:1, emerald on #ecfdf5 5.21:1 and on #d1fae5 4.84:1. Switch the focus ring from indigo #6366f1 (4.17:1 on ivory, and off-brand) to emerald or a darker shade, and check its 3:1 contrast against an emerald primary button. The ring's 2px offset over ivory handles this.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** (bundled at `css/fonts/`, 49 KB, not loaded yet) is wider than the system stack. The 380px tab bar already overflows by 36px (F15), and the 2-column decision grid at 380 is 155px per button. Check both after the swap. The CSS uses weights 550, 650 and 750, so confirm the woff2 files are variable or map those to the shipped weights. Add `font-display: swap` and a size-adjusted fallback so `file://` loads don't shift the layout.
- **GSAP** (`js/vendor/gsap.min.js` 73 KB plus `ScrollTrigger.min.js` 45 KB, and `js/motion.js`) is in the repo but not loaded. ScrollTrigger isn't needed for anything in these views, so leave it out. The views re-render with `innerHTML` on every store change, so tie animations to the selection changing or the view appearing, never to `render()`. Card transitions must not delay focus or block a fast A-A-A sequence: keep them at 200ms or less, interruptible, with focus set straight away. `motion.js` already returns early under reduced motion, so keep that.
- **The hero and notice** are the first thing a LinkedIn or hiring viewer sees. Settle the numbers (F07) before polishing them visually.
- Regenerate the favicon from the emerald token (F21).

Not checked: live mode in the browser (no API key; the setup form, progress view and hours slider were reviewed from code only), real screen-reader output (the ARIA was reviewed from code), and touch gestures on a physical device. On a fresh load, the first Tab in headless Chrome landed on the second queue row, which looks like a quirk of headless Chrome's focus starting point; after clicking the page, the order was correct (see Keep), so it isn't listed as a finding. The `design:accessibility-review` and `design:design-critique` skills weren't available in this session, so their checklists weren't applied; the UI UX Pro Max rules and WCAG 2.2 AA were used instead.
